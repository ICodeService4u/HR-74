# Offboarding Checklist

Whenever someone leaves Troutly — resignation, end of contract, or involuntary — we work this list top to bottom. Nothing here is optional. If a step doesn't apply, note why in the separation folder and move on. Questions go to People Ops.

## Who owns what

People Ops drives the checklist. Finance owns anything that touches pay. IT owns access and hardware. The departing person's manager owns the handoff of work, not the paperwork.

## The checklist

1. **Notify People Ops and Finance.** Email the Head of People (Anjelina Brocollini) and the Finance Manager (Krystale Jumawan) with the last day as soon as it's known. Same day if we can — payroll cutoffs move fast on the biweekly calendar.

2. **Enter the termination record in the HRIS.** People Ops enters the termination record in BambooHR effective the last day. A manager cannot enter a termination on their own record or on their own report without People Ops in the loop; the system-of-record entry belongs to People Ops.

3. **Open the IT deprovisioning ticket.** File the IT deprovisioning ticket the moment step 1 goes out. The ticket covers SSO, email, GitHub, cloud consoles, VPN, and any dept-specific tools the manager flags. IT will schedule disable-at-EOD on the last day unless we ask otherwise in writing.

4. **Track return of company equipment.** Laptop and badge return goes in the separation folder — serial numbers, condition notes, date received, who accepted it. Any additional hardware (monitors, keyboards, YubiKeys) goes on the same log. Unreturned equipment cost may be deducted from final pay ONLY where a signed Equipment Deduction Authorization form is in the folder for that employee, per Employee Handbook v3 section 11 and Texas Labor Code 61.018. No signed form on file, no deduction — full stop. If the form is missing, we pursue return through other means and note it in the file.

5. **Final pay calculation.** Finance runs an off-cycle final pay when the last day doesn't line up with the next regular check date. Sign-off by the Finance Manager is required on every final pay before release, off-cycle or not. People Ops sends the inputs (last day, PTO status, any approved deductions with backing paperwork) to Finance; Finance produces the calc and signs.

6. **PTO payout.** Accrued PTO is paid at separation only where the employee gave 14 calendar days written notice and worked through the notice period, per handbook 7.4. If either condition isn't met, no payout is owed. Rate and balance conventions live in the current PTO memo — don't invent a rate here.

7. **Schedule the exit interview.** People Ops books the exit interview for sometime in the last week. Thirty minutes, video or in-person. Notes go into the separation folder, not the HRIS.

8. **Send the COBRA notice.** People Ops notifies Lampasas Benefits Group of the qualifying event within 30 days of the last day, and the broker sends the election notice within 14 days of receiving it. Coverage runs through the end of the month containing the last day; the notice starts the election clock. People Ops confirms the broker sent it and files the confirmation.

## References

- Employee Handbook v3, section 7.4 — PTO payout at separation
- Employee Handbook v3, section 11 — Separation, wage-withholding rules, Equipment Deduction Authorization
- Employee Handbook v3, section 12 — Business expenses (recoveries at separation)
- Texas Labor Code 61.018 — written authorization required for wage deductions

## Separation folder contents

At close-out the folder should hold: signed resignation or termination memo, HRIS termination confirmation, IT deprovisioning ticket number and close-out, equipment return log, Equipment Deduction Authorization (if any), final pay calc with Finance Manager sign-off, exit interview notes, COBRA notice confirmation. If any of those are missing, the offboarding isn't done.
