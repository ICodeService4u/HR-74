We try to keep this page fresh, but the source of truth for reporting lines is always the current org chart PDF — see the link at the bottom.

## Who to ask about what

- Anything HR/benefits — talk to your manager first, then loop in the People function.
- Laptops, badges, desk moves, snacks, mail — Office Manager.
- Payroll timing, expense reimbursements, corporate card — Finance Manager.
- Recruiting pipeline, interview scheduling, offer questions — Talent.
- Wiki edits, page ownership, doc cleanup — anyone can edit; ping the page owner listed at the top of a page before rewriting large sections.
