# Benefits Overview

Eligibility: full-time employees from date of hire

## The basics

We offer medical, dental, and vision through Lampasas Benefits Group. Claudia Bong is our broker — she's the person to loop in when you have plan-specific questions. Our plan year runs September through August.

## Who's covered

If you're a full-time employee, you're eligible. You pick one of four coverage tiers when you enroll:

- Employee Only
- Employee plus Spouse
- Employee plus Children
- Family

If you don't want the coverage (e.g. you're on a spouse's plan), you can waive — just make sure the waiver is on file.

## What it costs

Below are the composite monthly premiums for the current plan year. These are the total premiums; Troutly pays 80% of the Employee Only premium for everyone who's enrolled, and the rest comes out of your paycheck pre-tax.

| Tier | Monthly premium |
|---|---|
| Employee Only | $571.90 |
| Employee plus Spouse | $1,199.50 |
| Employee plus Children | $1,086.30 |
| Family | $1,714.80 |

Rate sheet: /HR/Benefits/Rate_Sheet_2025.pdf

## Life events

Getting married, having a kid, adopting, losing other coverage — that's a qualifying life event and you can change your tier outside of open enrollment. Send the paperwork to People Ops within 30 days of the event.

## Open enrollment

Runs once a year in August. You'll get an email from the broker with the election form. If you don't send anything back, your current elections roll forward.

## Questions

Ping People Ops in Slack or email the broker directly. For anything plan-document-specific (deductibles, in-network questions, claims), Claudia is faster than we are.
