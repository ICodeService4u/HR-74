# Compensation Bands

Quick reference for our salary bands. We use these when we're building offers, checking a promotion, or just double-checking a compa-ratio sanity check. If a level you're looking at isn't here, ping People Ops.

## How we use them

We anchor to the midpoint of the level the person is actually working at. A hire coming in below midpoint is normal; above midpoint means we should have a good reason and a manager sign-off. Compa-ratio is salary divided by the midpoint of the current level.

## Bands

| Level | Midpoint |
|---|---|
| Customer Success Manager I | $90,000 |
| Customer Success Manager II | $108,000 |
| Senior Customer Success Manager | $112,000 |
| Account Executive | $92,000 |
| Software Engineer II | $121,000 |
| Senior Software Engineer | $144,000 |
| Support Specialist | $56,000 |
| Marketing Manager | $82,000 |
| Data Analyst II | $85,000 |

## Notes

- Support Specialists get a $3,000 annual step on their service anniversary. That's outside the band table and applies on top.
- Bands are base salary only. Sign-on bonuses, commission plans, and equity aren't in this table.

## When to escalate

If an offer is landing outside the band (above max or below min for a level), get the CEO or CTO on the approval line before we send it. Same for any mid-cycle base adjustment — signed approval from the manager AND CEO or CTO.
