### 3. Valuation & Rounding Standards
- **Hourly Rate:** Computed as `ROUND(Annual Base Salary / 2080, 4)` in accordance with Employee Handbook v3 Section 7.3, the PTO Policy Cutover Memo Section 4, and the July close workbook.
- **PTO Balance:** Carried at two decimal places (`ROUND(Hours, 2)`).
- **Dollar Liability:** Computed per employee as `ROUND(PTO Hours * Hourly Rate, 2)`.
- **Line & Total Liability:** Sum of rounded employee rows, tying to General Ledger Account 2140 (*Accrued PTO Liability*) for the August 31, 2026 balance sheet.
