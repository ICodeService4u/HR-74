## Reviewer decision rules, recorded in advance

Every row is read from the Wiki.js pages table and nothing else.

1. **The existence rows (1, 10)** pass when a page carrying the exact title, dashes normalised,
   is published. A draft fails.
2. **The Board approval rows (2, 3, 4, 5)** read the cell's leading token. Approved with a
   qualifier does not satisfy Not approved.
3. **The status rows (6, 7)** read the hiring status cell the same way.
4. **The summary rows (8, 9)** read the hiring page's prose. Row 8 passes on $612,000.00 within a
   sentence about the budget or the Board; row 9 on a statement that none or 0 of the approved
   roles is filled.
5. **The gate (11)** passes when the ID-keyed rows on the staffed tables are the roster's 52 and
   no other, and the summary states 52.
6. **The presence rows (12, 13)** read the department cell of the row keyed on the employee ID.
7. **The absence rows (14, 15, 16, 17)** pass when the ID sits on no staffed table that carries at
   least 40 keyed rows. Naming the person is not asked and not read.
8. **The department row (18)** passes when every department's count matches on the tables and is
   stated in the summary, in a sentence, a bulleted list or a table under the summary heading.
9. **The split rows (19, 20)** count keyed rows by the open requisition column; row 20 also fails
   a table that mixes requisition rows with none rows.
10. **The manager rows (21, 22)** pass when the manager cell carries the surname or the ID.
11. **The form rows (23, 24)** pass when every Board approval cell opens on one of the two values,
    and when no ISO, spelled or day-first date appears on either page.
