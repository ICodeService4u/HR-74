## Major issues

### [P1] Verifiers Grade Only What the Prompt Asks For

Five verifiers grade unrequested calculation-path explanations. In the task-file memo, the page
is limited to rows with "employee ID, name, department, annual PTO tier in hours, PTO balance in
hours at 08/31/2026, hourly rate, and dollar liability," plus the stated summary; it does not ask
for service-date bridging, accrual breakdowns, posting-period counts, or leave-deduction
explanations. Remove those extra conditions from criteria 8, 9, 16, 17, and 18, retaining only
the directly requested table values.

### [P0] Prompt-Rubric Alignment

The attached request requires specific page structure and fields that the rubric does not grade.
It says, "One page published in Wiki.js, PTO Liability - 08/31/2026, with a short summary above
one table," and "One table with a row for every current employee: employee ID, name, department,
annual PTO tier in hours, PTO balance in hours at 08/31/2026, hourly rate, and dollar liability";
criteria 2-4 grade the population, selected numeric presentation, and the summary's contents, but
no verifier requires the employee ID, name, department, or annual-tier column for every row, or
enforces the summary-above-a-single-table layout. The same memo explicitly says "Change nothing
in Greenhouse," but none of criteria 1-21 checks that prohibition. Add verifiers for every
required column and layout constraint, and a snapshot-based check that Greenhouse data is
unchanged.

### [P0] No Stacked Criteria (Atomicity)

The rubric stacks independently gradable employee outcomes into single criteria. For example,
criterion 6 requires five separate balances, and criterion 19 requires correct policies for six
separate employees; a response can be right for some employees and wrong for others, yet each
criterion produces only one result. Criteria 20 and 21 also overlap: the universal BambooHR
balance requirement includes TRT-0153 and TRT-0155, while criterion 21 separately asks for their
balance rows. Split each listed multi-employee criterion into employee-level checks and remove or
narrow the duplicate coverage check.

Finding #1, MAJOR. Criteria 6 and 7 bundle five independent employee facts each. Criterion 6
requires five separately verifiable employee balances in one check, and criterion 7 similarly
requires the correct PTO tier for five distinct employees. The entries are weighted 5 and have no
dependencies, so this is not the low-weight related-value exception; split each employee result
into its own criterion.

Finding #2, MAJOR. Criteria 15 and 16 combine separate employee outcomes. Criterion 15 requires
exclusion of four named employees in one result, while criterion 16 requires both tier/accrual
treatment for two separate employees. Each person can be correct or incorrect independently;
separate these employee-level requirements into atomic criteria.

Finding #3, MAJOR. Criterion 19 combines six independent BambooHR policy updates. The criterion
requires the schedule-derived policy for each of six employees whose loaded policy differed. Those
policy assignments are independently gradable and not one contractual element, so replace this
bundled criterion with separate checks for each affected employee.

Finding #4, MAJOR. Criteria 20 and 21 overlap, and criterion 21 also stacks two rows. Criterion
20 requires a matching PTO balance for every scheduled employee, which includes TRT-0153 and
TRT-0155; criterion 21 repeats their required balance-row coverage. Criterion 21 additionally
combines the two separately checkable rows, so remove the duplicate or make the universal check
explicitly exclude any separately scored additions and split the two row checks.

### [P0] Rubric Completeness

The rubric exists, but all 21 judge-graded entries are missing the required Reference Artifacts
and Grading Target fields. In `mcp/verifiers.json`, each affected verifier has
`verifier_custom_field_values = {}`; for example, the enumeration shows this for the first entry,
`ver_f1bddc57396740dca93834464359ea73`, and the final entry,
`ver_5f4a1e594e98458e9bef422b9078bb10`, as well as every entry between them. These omissions
violate the completeness requirements for judge-graded verifiers; populate both fields on each
entry.

## Minor issues

### [P1] Rubric Design - Criteria Count Justified by Scope

The criterion set is padded by one redundant check. In `mcp/verifiers.json`, C20 requires a "PTO
balance equal to the schedule's 08/31/2026 balance for every employee on the schedule," while C21
requires only "a PTO balance row for TRT-0153 and TRT-0155"; those two employees are part of the
schedule, so C20 necessarily covers their rows and balances. Remove C21 or fold its new-hire
rationale into C20 rather than awarding a separate criterion.

### Verifier Tag Coverage

All 21 graded criteria in `mcp/verifiers.json` are untagged. The exhaustive inspection reports
`counts: 21 with code 0 with tags key 0`, while each entry is identified as a judge-style
`Objective Compliance` or `Expert Assessment` criterion, not a programmatic verifier. This breaks
the required classifier coverage and downstream reporting; add an approved, criterion-appropriate
tag to every listed criterion.
